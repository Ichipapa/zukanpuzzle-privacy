Jigsaw Japan Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Jigsaw Japan" (Japanese name: ジグソー図鑑, the "App") handles information about its users.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information, such as your name, email address, phone number or location. There is no account registration.

The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Your puzzle progress (the positions of placed pieces and the pictures you have finished)
  • Your collection (the prefectures you have collected, the fun fact cards you have read, and your quiz stamps)

2. Ads and Analytics

The App does not show ads. It does not use any third-party analytics services, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. In-App Purchases

If you buy a paid pack, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple manages your payment information, and we never receive it. A parental check is shown before any purchase.

4. Sharing With Third Parties

Because the App does not collect information about you, we have nothing to share with third parties.

5. Children

The App is designed to be enjoyed by both adults and children. As described above, it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when needed. Any changes will be posted on this page.
